🎨 Pixel-Art Toy

  • Type what you want to see in the Describe your image box.
  • Style picks a color palette (or Auto, chosen from your words). Caption adds optional poster text.
  • Enhance my prompt with Signature Llama lets the AI expand your idea first — if Llama can't load, the on-device engine is used instead and the result says so.
  • Draw renders the retro picture. Surprise me fills in a random idea.
  • After a render you get a result record (prompt, settings, seed — the same input always makes the same art), plus Download PNG, Download SVG, Regenerate, Copy prompt + settings, and Read description.
  • 🛡️ Trademark-safe: naming a trademarked character renders an original Signature-style abstraction instead — and says so.

🎬 Pixel Video Toy

  • Describe the scene, pick a length (3, 6 or 10 seconds), optional caption, then Animate — the retro animation previews live.
  • Download .webm exports the video. .webm export needs Chrome or Edge on desktop — the live preview plays in any modern browser.
