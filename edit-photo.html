❓ How to use the Photo Shop
  1. Open a photo — yours, the sample, or any good/ad via “✏️ Open in Photo Shop”.
  2. Filters: slide Brightness / Contrast / Color, or tap a look (B&W, Sepia, Vintage, Vivid).
  3. Crop: drag a box, tap “Cut to this box”.
  4. Turn / Flip rotate and mirror the photo.
  5. Words: type, Place, drag them where you want, then “Stamp it down” to bake them in.
  6. Brush: pick a color and size, paint on the photo.
  7. ↩ Undo steps back one change at a time. Download saves PNG or JPG.
