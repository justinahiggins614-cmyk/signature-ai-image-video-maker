❓ How to use this editor
  1. Open a video from your phone, or try the sample clip.
  2. Trim: drag the gold handles on the timeline. Split: move the playhead and tap ✂ — then tap a clip and 🗑 to cut a part out.
  3. Fade: move the playhead, set Opacity, tap ＋ Keyframe. Add two (e.g. 0% then 100%) for a fade-in. Drag the pink diamonds to move them.
  4. Words: ＋ Add text, type, Place it, then drag the words right on the video.
  5. Music: ＋ Add audio — it starts where the playhead is.
  6. Export .webm when it looks right. Needs Chrome or Edge; other browsers may not support recording.
